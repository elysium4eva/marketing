Chapter outline

Part 1Quality overview: the five characteristics (validity, accuracy, completeness, consistency, uniformity of units), grain, layering and data contracts, clean vs transform vs reshape, five rules for reproducible preparation
Part 2Combine and reshape: append / concat, the four join types and join cardinality, wide ↔ long layouts, pivot / melt / stack / explode / cut
Part 3Data cleaning: what cleaning is worth, the five cleaning steps (duplicates, structural errors, outliers, missing values, validation) and the cleaning log
Part 4Missing data: where missingness comes from, MCAR / MAR / MNAR, imputation strategies and their cost
Part 5Panels: balanced vs unbalanced panels, cleaning and interpolating, Stata against pandas
Part 6Transform and tools: variable transformations, standardisation, the toolbox and reproducible pipelines
Part 7Project and wrap-up: the one-page proposal, the quality report template, the 30-item checklist, glossary and references

Learning objectives

  • Describe and quantify the quality of a dataset along five characteristics: validity, accuracy, completeness, consistency and uniformity of units;
  • Use append / concat (adding observations) and merge / join (adding variables) correctly, work out the join cardinality and verify that the key is unique;
  • Move freely between wide and long layouts, and use pivot / melt / stack / explode / cut fluently;
  • Apply the five cleaning steps to duplicates, structural errors, outliers, missing values and validation, and keep an auditable cleaning log;
  • Recognise the three missingness mechanisms MCAR / MAR / MNAR, choose and evaluate an imputation strategy, and state what imputation costs;
  • Diagnose panel balance, complete the variable transformations and write a reproducible preparation pipeline.
