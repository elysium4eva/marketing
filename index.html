Final project

A team selects a Chinese brand, product or service for entry into another country or region — or a foreign offering for entry into China — and develops and evaluates an innovative international marketing strategy, including a cross-border e-commerce channel portfolio. At least one data analytic tool and one AI agent workflow must be used and documented.

AI use disclosure

Every submission must include an AI-use appendix: the tool and model used, the workflow or prompts, the inputs, the material outputs used, the verification process, corrections, limitations, and the human decisions. Unverified AI-generated evidence is treated as academic misconduct.
